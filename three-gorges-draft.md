# Three Gorges Project — Case Study Page Draft

## Section 1: Project Background

**Headline:** Powering China's Greatest Water Conservancy Project

**Body:**
In 1999, the Three Gorges Project — the world's largest hydropower facility and a national strategic key project of China — called upon our company as one of its critical equipment suppliers. As a pioneer in China's hydraulic hoist industry, we were entrusted with supplying hydraulic hoists for the dam's flood discharge system, intake gates, and sand discharge outlets.

The Three Gorges Dam, spanning the Yangtze River in Hubei Province, was designed to harness the power of one of the world's greatest rivers for flood control, clean energy generation, and navigation improvement.

---

## Section 2: Project Scale

**Headline:** Engineering at an Unprecedented Scale

**Body:**
The scope of our involvement covered three critical systems of the dam:

| System | Quantity | Equipment |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Flood Discharge Tunnels | 23 | QHSY Hydraulic Hoists |
| Intake Openings | 14 | QPKY Hydraulic Hoists |
| Sand Discharge Outlets | — | Hydraulic Hoists |

**Key Technical Specifications:**

**Intake Rapid Gates (Left & Right Bank Powerhouse)**
- Type: QPKY Hydraulic Hoist
- Cylinder Bore: 710 mm
- Stroke: 15 m

**Flood Discharge Tunnels**
- Type: QHSY Hydraulic Hoist
- Cylinder Bore: 650 mm
- Stroke: 11.29 m

---

## Section 3: Our Solution

**Headline:** Domestic Innovation Meets National-Grade Demands

**Body:**
For the Three Gorges Project, we engineered and delivered two specialized hydraulic hoist systems:

- **QPKY-type** hydraulic hoists for the intake rapid gates on both the left and right bank powerhouses — designed for fast, reliable gate closure under extreme hydraulic pressure.
- **QHSY-type** hydraulic hoists for the flood discharge tunnels — built to handle the rigorous operating conditions of high-flow water release.

Every unit was manufactured to meet the stringent requirements of the Three Gorges Project, including precise stroke control, high-load capacity, and long-term operational reliability in a demanding hydraulic environment.

---

## Section 4: Acceptance & Compliance

**Headline:** Meeting the Highest Standards

**Body:**
The project was officially completed in **June 2014** and successfully passed the final acceptance inspection.

Through independent third-party testing and verification, all products were confirmed to fully comply with national standards. The system has demonstrated stable operation and high processing efficiency, ensuring long-term compliance with all applicable regulatory requirements.

---

## Section 5: Project Significance

**Headline:** A Milestone for China's Heavy Engineering

**Body:**
The successful delivery of hydraulic hoists for the Three Gorges Project marked the **first large-scale application of domestically manufactured hydraulic hoist equipment** in a national strategic key project.

This achievement demonstrated that Chinese-made heavy engineering equipment could meet the most demanding specifications — paving the way for domestic suppliers to compete in projects of national and global significance.
