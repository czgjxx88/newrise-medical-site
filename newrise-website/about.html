Who We Are

Changzhou Newrise Medical & Hygiene Products Co., Ltd

Changzhou Newrise Medical & Hygiene Products Co., Ltd is a professional manufacturer in the medical and healthcare industry, specializing in producing a wide range of disposable products. Established in 2004 and located in Changzhou, our facility covers an area of 40,000m2 which includes a controlled workshop area of 20,000m2 and a Class 100,000 cleanroom area of 1,500m2.

We operate six major product lines: washgloves, tissue and PE laminated products, oral swabs, incontinence reusable underpads, emergency goods, and epidemic prevention supplies. Our products are exported to Europe, the USA, the Middle East, and Japan, serving customers in over 50 countries worldwide.

Core Product Lines
