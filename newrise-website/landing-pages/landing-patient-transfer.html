Safe, Efficient Patient Handling

Patient transfer is one of the most physically demanding tasks for healthcare workers. Newrise Medical's patient transfer and slide sheets are designed to make repositioning, transferring, and mobilizing patients safer and easier.

Made from low-friction, medical-grade fabrics, our products reduce the force required for patient handling by up to 70%, significantly lowering the risk of caregiver back injuries and improving patient dignity and comfort during transfers.
