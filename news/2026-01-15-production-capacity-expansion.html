Newrise Medical Expands Production Capacity in 2026

Adding 10 new automated production lines to our 40,000㎡ Changzhou facility — a 30% increase in annual output to meet surging global demand for disposable medical and hygiene products.

A Milestone Investment in Manufacturing Excellence

Changzhou Newrise Medical & Hygiene Products Co., Ltd. is proud to announce a major expansion of its production capabilities in early 2026. The company has installed 10 new automated production lines at its 40,000㎡ manufacturing facility in Changzhou, Jiangsu Province, representing one of the largest capacity investments in the company's 22-year history.

This expansion brings Newrise Medical's total production lines to over 40, significantly increasing our ability to serve distributors, healthcare procurement teams, and OEM partners across more than 50 countries. The investment underscores our commitment to remaining a reliable, high-volume supplier in the global disposable medical products market.

"This expansion isn't just about making more products — it's about making more products at the same uncompromising quality standard our customers expect." — Newrise Medical Management Team

What the Expansion Includes

The 10 new production lines are designed for efficiency, precision, and scalability. Each line features state-of-the-art automation technology that reduces manual handling, improves consistency, and accelerates throughput. Here's a breakdown of the new capabilities:

Why Now? Understanding the Market Dynamics

The decision to expand was driven by several converging market trends that have accelerated demand for disposable medical and hygiene products worldwide:

1. Growing Global Healthcare Demand

The aging global population and increasing awareness of infection prevention have driven sustained demand for single-use medical products. The global disposable medical supplies market is projected to exceed $150 billion by 2028, with Asia-Pacific being the fastest-growing region.

2. Post-Pandemic Infection Control Standards

Healthcare facilities worldwide have permanently elevated their infection control protocols. Single-use products — from wash gloves to patient transfer sheets — are now standard practice in hospitals that previously relied on reusable alternatives. This shift has created structural, long-term demand growth.

3. OEM Partner Expansion

Newrise Medical has seen a 40% year-over-year increase in OEM and private-label inquiries from distributors in North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia. The new production lines provide the flexibility and capacity to handle these growing partnership opportunities.

Quality at Scale: The ISO 13485 Commitment

Capacity means nothing without quality. Newrise Medical operates under an ISO 13485:2016 certified quality management system, ensuring that every product — regardless of which production line it comes from — meets the same rigorous standards. Our quality control processes include:

  1. Raw material inspection — All incoming materials are tested against specifications before entering production
  2. In-process quality checks — Automated sensors and manual inspections at every stage of the production process
  3. Final product testing — Every batch is tested for dimensions, absorbency, strength, and sterility (where applicable) before release
  4. Traceability — Full batch-level traceability from raw material to finished product shipment

This commitment to quality is reflected in our CE marking and FDA registration, which are maintained across all product categories and production lines.

What This Means for Our Customers

For existing and prospective customers, this capacity expansion delivers several tangible benefits:

  • Shorter lead times — Increased capacity means faster turnaround on orders, especially during peak demand periods
  • Competitive pricing — Economies of scale from larger production runs translate to better pricing for our partners
  • Greater product availability — Reduced risk of stock-outs and backorders on high-demand items
  • Enhanced OEM capabilities — The new flexible production line enables faster prototyping and shorter sample lead times for custom products
  • Consistent quality — More automated processes mean less variability and more consistent product performance

Looking Ahead: 2026 and Beyond

This production expansion is part of a broader growth strategy that includes continued investment in R&D, sustainability initiatives, and global market expansion. Newrise Medical is actively pursuing new product development in areas such as eco-friendly disposable products and advanced infection control solutions.

With over two decades of manufacturing experience, a modern facility, and now significantly expanded capacity, Newrise Medical is well-positioned to serve as a trusted, long-term supply partner for healthcare facilities and distributors worldwide.
