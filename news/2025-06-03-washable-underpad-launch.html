Newrise Launches Eco-Friendly Washable Underpad Line

A sustainable, cost-effective alternative to disposable underpads — with TPU waterproof backing designed for 300+ wash cycles, combining performance with environmental responsibility.

A Step Toward Sustainable Incontinence Care

Newrise Medical is proud to announce the launch of our new washable underpad series — a sustainable, high-performance alternative to traditional disposable bed pads. Designed for healthcare facilities, nursing homes, and home care settings, this product line addresses two growing demands in the medical supply industry: environmental responsibility and cost efficiency.

The global healthcare sector generates enormous waste from single-use products. Disposable underpads alone account for millions of tons of landfill waste annually. Our new washable line offers facilities a way to dramatically reduce their environmental footprint without sacrificing the protection, comfort, or hygiene standards their patients and residents expect.

"We've spent over a year developing this product line. The challenge wasn't just making it washable — it was making it washable 300 times while maintaining the same level of absorbency, comfort, and waterproof performance as our disposable underpads." — Product Development Team, Newrise Medical

Product Features: What Makes It Different

Our reusable bed pad line is engineered with three distinct layers, each serving a specific function:

Top Layer: Soft Cotton Surface

The surface layer features a cotton-blend fabric that feels gentle against the skin. This is particularly important for elderly patients and those with sensitive skin conditions, where rough or synthetic surfaces can cause irritation, pressure sores, or discomfort during extended use. The breathable cotton top allows air circulation, reducing moisture buildup and skin maceration risk.

Middle Layer: High-Absorbency Core

The absorbent core combines fluff pulp with a measured amount of super-absorbent polymer (SAP) to quickly wick moisture away from the surface and lock it in. This dual-action absorption ensures that liquids don't pool on the surface — a critical feature for maintaining skin health and patient dignity.

Bottom Layer: TPU Waterproof Backing

The defining feature of this product line is the TPU waterproof backing. Unlike traditional PE film backing — which can crack, peel, and degrade after repeated washing — thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) maintains its waterproof integrity through hundreds of wash cycles. Key advantages include:

  • 300+ wash cycles without compromising waterproof performance
  • Flexible and quiet — no crinkling noise that disturbs patients
  • Eco-friendly material — TPU is recyclable and free from PVC, phthalates, and chlorine
  • Breathable yet waterproof — prevents condensation buildup underneath the pad

Environmental Impact

Beyond cost savings, the environmental benefits of switching to a reusable bed pad system are significant:

  1. Landfill reduction: A single 100-bed facility using disposable underpads generates approximately 109,500 pads per year. At an average weight of 50g per pad, that's over 5.4 tons of non-biodegradable waste annually.
  2. Carbon footprint: Manufacturing, packaging, and shipping single-use products generates substantially more CO₂ per use than washing and reusing a durable product.
  3. Sustainability credentials: Facilities transitioning to washable products can highlight their environmental commitment in marketing materials, accreditation reports, and ESG disclosures — increasingly important for funding and regulatory compliance.
